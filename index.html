Murree Trip Itinerary

Hotel: Cecil Hotel by Pearl Continental (Mount View Road, near Mall Road)
Dates: 23–28 November 2026 (travel days + 4 full days)
Group: 6 people (you, fiancée, mom, her sister, two cousins) — two cars from Lahore
Focus: Shopping + sightseeing for all; easy walks for everyone; optional moderate hikes for half the group

Hotel note: Historic 1851 colonial heritage property, restored, with period furniture and fireplaces. Central location walking distance to Mall Road. Confirm rooms (Heritage or Studio wing) and any family suite options in advance.

Weather Expectation (Late November)

  • Daytime highs: roughly 10–15°C (50–59°F)
  • Nighttime lows: 0–5°C (32–41°F), can feel colder with wind
  • Possible light rain, frost, or light snow on higher slopes (main snow season is Dec–Feb)
  • Cool, crisp air; good for walking but pack layers
  • Check forecast closer to travel (roads can be affected if it rains or freezes)

Shops & Mall Road

Mall Road shops (woollens, dry fruits, handicrafts, souvenirs, clothes) are generally open year-round. Typical hours ~9 AM–10 PM. Late November is quieter than peak summer/winter snow season, so more relaxed shopping. Main tourist shops stay open; some smaller stalls may close earlier or have reduced hours. Kashmir Point area also has shops.

Packing List (Brief)

  • Layers: thermal/base layer, fleece or wool sweater, waterproof/windproof jacket
  • Warm trousers (avoid thin jeans if rain possible), sturdy closed shoes with good grip
  • Wool socks, gloves, scarf/cap, light backpack
  • Sunscreen + sunglasses (UV still present at altitude), lip balm, moisturizer
  • Basic medicines, power bank, reusable water bottle
  • For cars: chains not usually needed in late Nov but check road status; snacks and water for drive

5-Day Itinerary

Day 1 — Mon 23 Nov: Travel & Arrival

  • Depart Lahore morning (Motorway → Islamabad → Murree Expressway; ~5–6 hours depending on stops/traffic).
  • Arrive Cecil Hotel, check-in, settle in (heritage vibe + fireplaces).
  • Evening: Short walk on Mall Road if energy allows — lights, food stalls, first look at shops. Or rest at hotel (Marco Polo restaurant / Front Page Café on site).
  • Dinner at hotel or nearby.

Day 2 — Tue 24 Nov: Mall Road Shopping + Kashmir Point

  • Morning/afternoon: Mall Road shopping (mom, sister, girl cousin focus). Woollens, dry fruits, handicrafts, souvenirs. Walkable from hotel.
  • All group: Easy walk to Kashmir Point (viewpoint toward hills/Kashmir). Quiet, good for photos and nature. Short nature trail possible.
  • Optional: Hotel heritage tour if available.
  • Evening: More shopping or hotel dinner. Flexible pace.

Easy for everyone. Split if some want more shopping time.

Day 3 — Wed 25 Nov: Patriata (New Murree) Chairlift

  • Drive ~20–30 min to Patriata (New Murree).
  • Chairlift + cable car ride through pine forest to top station — great views, fun for the whole group (easy, no major hiking required).
  • Light walking at the top for views and photos.
  • Return afternoon. Optional stop for snacks.
  • Evening free or light Mall Road visit.

Sightseeing highlight for all. Check chairlift operating status closer to date (usually runs year-round).

Day 4 — Thu 26 Nov: Pindi Point + Optional Hike / Flexible

  • Morning: Pindi Point views (toward Rawalpindi/Islamabad) + optional short chairlift ride. Easy for everyone.
  • Option A (easier): More shopping or rest at hotel / café time.
  • Option B (moderate for half the group): Day trip toward Ayubia or Nathia Gali area (~45–60 min drive) for Pipeline Track or easy forest walks (flat-to-moderate trails through pines). Others can stay in Murree or join for shorter version.
  • Evening: Group dinner.

Natural split point — half can do moderate hike, others shop or relax.

Day 5 — Fri 27 Nov: Free Day / Nature & Views

  • Flexible morning: Easy nature walk near hotel or back to a viewpoint (Kashmir or Pindi Point).
  • More shopping if desired (Mall Road or side areas).
  • Optional short drive to Bhurban area for quieter views (golf course nearby, but mainly scenic).
  • Afternoon: Pack lightly, last café stop, hotel downtime.
  • Evening: Final dinner together.

Day 6 — Sat 28 Nov: Departure

  • Check-out and drive back to Lahore (allow 5–6+ hours). Leave mid-morning for comfortable arrival.

Practical Notes

  • Two cars: good for flexibility if group splits for hikes vs shopping.
  • Parking in Murree can be tight; hotel parking available (confirm).
  • Altitude ~2,300 m — stay hydrated, go easy first day if anyone feels the elevation.
  • Road status: check TDCP / district updates before departure, especially if rain is forecast.
  • Food: Hotel restaurants + plenty of options on Mall Road (roast chicken, Kashmiri chai, local snacks).

Itinerary is flexible — adjust based on weather, energy, and group preference. Late November is quieter and pleasant for walking and shopping compared to peak seasons.
